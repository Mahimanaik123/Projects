About Him

A Life of Inspiration

1931 – 2015

Dr. Avul Pakir Jainulabdeen Abdul Kalam was one of India's most respected scientists and statesmen. He played a significant role in India's space and defence programmes.

He worked with organizations such as ISRO and DRDO and contributed to India's missile development and space research programmes.

In 2002, he became the 11th President of India. Known as the "People's President", he was especially admired by students and young people for his simple lifestyle, humility and vision for India's future.

His Contributions

Missile Development

Played an important role in India's missile development programmes, including Agni and Prithvi.

02

SLV-III

Served as Project Director for India's Satellite Launch Vehicle-III programme.

03

President of India

Served as the 11th President of India from 2002 to 2007.

04

Bharat Ratna

Received India's highest civilian award, the Bharat Ratna, in 1997.

05

Author

Authored inspirational books including "Wings of Fire" and "Ignited Minds".

06

Youth Inspiration

Dedicated much of his later life to motivating students and encouraging scientific thinking.

Journey

Life Timeline

Some important milestones in his remarkable life.

1931 — Born

Born on October 15, 1931, in Rameswaram, Tamil Nadu.

1960s — Joined India's Space Programme

Began his professional journey in India's space research and aerospace programmes.

1980 — SLV-III Success

The SLV-III successfully placed the Rohini satellite into near-Earth orbit.

1997 — Bharat Ratna

Awarded the Bharat Ratna for his exceptional contribution to the nation.

2002 — President of India

Became the 11th President of India and served until 2007.

2015 — Passed Away

Passed away on July 27, 2015, while delivering a lecture to students at IIM Shillong.

His Legacy Lives On

Dr. Kalam's life reminds us that knowledge, hard work and determination can transform dreams into reality. His message to students continues to inspire generations across India and the world.
